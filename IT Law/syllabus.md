## Course Structure & Modules

### Module 1: Introduction to the Information Technology Act, 2000
- Historical context, UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce (1996)
- Major Amendments to the IT Act (including 2008 Amendment)
- Scope and Extraterritorial Application (Section 1 & Section 75)
- Key Definitions: Data, Computer System, Electronic Record, Intermediary, Digital/Electronic Signatures (Section 2)

### Module 2: Liability of Intermediaries & Platform Governance
- Safe Harbor Protection under Section 79 of the IT Act, 2000
- Shreya Singhal v. Union of India standards and "actual knowledge"
- Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 & Amendment Rules 2023
- Due diligence obligations, grievance redressal, and content takedown mechanisms

### Module 3: Cyber-Speech & Regulation of Online Information
- Freedom of Speech and Expression in Cyberspace (Article 19(1)(a) & reasonable restrictions under 19(2))
- Legal Regulation of Fake News, Misinformation, and Disinformation
- Criminal and civil boundaries of online expression

### Module 4: Cybersecurity Framework & Incident Reporting
- National Cyber Security Policy 2013
- Statutory Provisions: Protected Systems (Sec. 70), National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre / NCIIPC (Sec. 70A), and Indian Computer Emergency Response Team / CERT-In (Sec. 70B)
- Information Technology (CERT-In Rules 2013 & Directions 2022/2018)

### Module 5: Data Protection & Comparative Frameworks (EU GDPR)
- Evolution of Information Privacy & European Union Data Protection Architecture
- EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation 2016/679)
- Principles of Data Processing: Lawfulness, fairness, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity, and confidentiality
- Rights of Data Subjects: Right of access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), and data portability
- Cross-border data transfers and standard contractual clauses

### Module 6: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023
- Scope of the law
- Select definitions
- Obligations of data fiduciary
- Rights of data principal
- Transfer of data to outside India
- Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025

### Module 7: Cyber Contraventions & Cyber Crimes
- Civil Contraventions and Adjudication (Section 43 & Section 43A)
- Tampering with computer source documents (Section 65)
- Computer related offenses & Hacking (Section 66)
- Identity theft & Cheating by personation (Sections 66C & 66D)
- Privacy violation / Voyeurism (Section 66E)
- Cyber terrorism (Section 66F)
- Publishing or transmitting obscene / sexually explicit material (Sections 67, 67A, 67B)

## Landmark Cases
- **Intermediary Liability:** *Super Cassettes Industries v. MySpace Inc.*; *Swami Ramdev v. Facebook Inc.*; *Amazon Seller Services v. Modicare Ltd.*; *Christian Louboutin v. Nakul Bajaj*; *Flipkart Internet v. State of NCT of Delhi*; *Neetu Singh v. Telegram*; *Indiamart Intermesh v. Puma SE*.
- **Free Speech & Regulation:** *Shreya Singhal v. Union of India*; *Arnab Ranjan Goswami v. UOI*; *Kunal Kamra v. UOI*; *Mohammed Zubair v. State of NCT of Delhi*.
- **Cyber-Obscenity:** *Ekta Kapoor v. State of M.P.*; *Apoorva Arora v. State*; *Just Rights for Children Alliance v. S. Harish*.
- **Data Protection & Privacy:** *K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India*; *Big Brother Watch v. UK*; *Schrems v. Data Protection Commissioner*; *Data Protection Commissioner v. Facebook Ireland*; *Google Spain v. AEPD*.
