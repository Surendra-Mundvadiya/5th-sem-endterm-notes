# Principles of Taxation Law - Course Syllabus

**Course Code:** IP61015  
**Course Coordinator:** Dr. Neeraj Kumar Gupta  
**Credits:** 3-1-0 (4 Credits)  
**Institution:** Rajiv Gandhi School of Intellectual Property Law, IIT Kharagpur  

---

## Course Learning Outcomes
Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Explain the sources of government power to impose taxes under constitutional provisions.
- Articulate the economic rationale, distinctions, and incidence of taxation.
- Interpret and analyze the provisions governing the five heads of income under the Income Tax Act.
- Compute deductions, exemptions, and total taxable income.
- Critically evaluate principles of tax administration, assessment procedures, and dispute adjudication.

---

## Detailed Course Units

### Unit I: Introduction to Taxation (4 Hours)
- History, purpose, types, moral dilemmas, economic impact, and incidence of taxation
- Taxation and macroeconomic governance
- Distinction among Tax, Cess, and Fee (*Delhi Race Club Ltd. v. Union of India*)

### Unit II: Constitutional Dimensions & Interpretation of Tax Statutes (3 Hours)
- Constitutional power to levy tax: Union List, State List, Concurrent List (Articles 245, 246, 265)
- Financial relations between Union and States (Part XII, Articles 264–300A)
- Fundamental rights and tax legislation (Articles 14, 19(1)(g))
- Institutional roles of the Finance Commission and NITI Aayog
- Rules of strict construction of taxing statutes

### Unit III: Core Concepts & Principles of Income Tax (8 Hours)
- Conceptual definitions: Income, Capital vs Revenue Receipts, Application vs Diversion of Income (*CIT v. Sitaldas Tirathdas*)
- Doctrine of Mutuality (*Bangalore Club v. CIT*)
- Accrual vs Arisal of income; Assessment Year, Previous Year, and Tax Year
- Scope of Total Income & Residential Status (Resident, RNOR, Non-Resident)
- Agricultural Income and Section 10 exemptions

### Unit IV: Income Under the Head "Salaries" (6 Hours)
- Chargeability under Section 15; employer-employee relationship
- Salary, allowances, perquisites (Section 17), and profits in lieu of salary
- Permissible deductions from salary (Standard deduction, entertainment allowance, professional tax)

### Unit V: Income from House Property (4 Hours)
- Basis of charge (Section 22); ownership, deemed owner (Section 27)
- Computation of Annual Value (Gross Annual Value, municipal taxes, Net Annual Value)
- Allowable statutory deductions under Section 24 (Standard 30% deduction and interest on borrowed capital)

### Unit VI: Profits and Gains of Business or Profession (PGBP) (6 Hours)
- Principles for computing business profits (Sections 28 & 29)
- General and specific deductions (Depreciation under Section 32, tea/coffee development, scientific research, bad debts)
- Disallowance of expenses (Section 40, Section 40A) and presumptive taxation schemes

### Unit VII: Capital Gains (4 Hours)
- Scope and chargeability (Section 45)
- Definition of "Capital Asset" [Sec. 2(14)] and "Transfer" [Sec. 2(47)]
- Short-term vs Long-term capital assets; indexed cost of acquisition and computation mechanics
- Exemptions from capital gains (Sections 54, 54B, 54EC, 54F)

### Unit VIII: Income from Other Sources (4 Hours)
- Residual head of income (Section 56)
- Taxation of dividends, interest, winnings from lotteries/crossword puzzles/horse races
- Gifts and receipt of money/property without consideration or for inadequate consideration [Sec. 56(2)(x)]
- Clubbing of income provisions (Sections 60 to 64)

### Unit IX: Assessment, Authorities & Adjudication (8 Hours)
- Return of income (filing deadlines, belated/revised returns)
- Types of Assessment: Self-assessment, Summary assessment, Scrutiny assessment, Best Judgment Assessment (Section 144)
- Hierarchy, powers, and functions of Income Tax Authorities (CBDT, CIT, Assessing Officers)
- Dispute resolution, rectification, appeals, and revision mechanisms (CIT(Appeals), ITAT, High Court, Supreme Court)
- Relief against Double Taxation (Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements - DTAA)
