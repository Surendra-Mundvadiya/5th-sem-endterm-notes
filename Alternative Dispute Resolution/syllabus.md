# Alternative Dispute Resolution - Course Syllabus

**Course Teacher:** Dr. S. R. Subramanian  
**Institution:** Rajiv Gandhi School of Intellectual Property Law, IIT Kharagpur  

---

## 1. Introduction
- **Advantages of ADR / Arbitration**
- **Different Modes of Arbitration**
- **Arbitration Agreement:** Applicable law, party autonomy
- **Extent of Judicial Intervention**
- **Statutory Framework:** Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996, Arbitration Act 1940, Foreign Awards (Recognition and Enforcement) Act
- **Institutional and Ad-hoc Arbitration:** UNCITRAL Model Law on International Commercial Arbitration
- **Statutory Arbitration & International Commercial Arbitration**
- **Arbitrators:** Duties, powers, and termination of arbitrators
- **Arbitral Award:** Types of awards, remedies, and relief
- **Public Policy & Appeals**
- **Enforcement of Awards:** New York Convention (1958), Geneva Convention and Protocols

---

## 2. Indian Law of Arbitration
- **Arbitrability:** Domestic arbitration vs. International Commercial Arbitration; Indian judicial standards on arbitrability
- **Arbitration Agreement:** Validity, writing requirement, waiver
- **Scope of Judicial Intervention:** Power to refer parties to arbitration, interim measures (Sections 9 & 17), composition of arbitral tribunal, appointment of arbitrators, grounds of challenge, jurisdiction (competence-competence), and conduct of arbitral proceedings

---

## 3. Conciliation
- **History and Conceptual Framework of Conciliation**
- **Conciliators:** Number, appointment, role, and rights of conciliators
- **Procedure and Advantages of Conciliation**
- **Conciliation under the CPC:** Section 89 and Order X
- **Settlement Agreement and Status:** UNCITRAL Conciliation Rules (1980)

---

## 4. Mediation
- **Essence and Benefits of Mediation**
- **Mediation Process:** Stages, caucus, joint sessions
- **Role of the Mediator:** Facilitative vs. evaluative mediation
- **Litigation vs. Mediation**
- **Tools and Techniques:** Active listening, reframing, reality testing, caucus
- **Termination and Settlement**

---

## 5. Lok Adalat
- **Meaning and Goals of Access to Justice**
- **Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987:** Permanent Lok Adalats, jurisdiction, award finality
- **The Gram Nyayalayas Act, 2008**
