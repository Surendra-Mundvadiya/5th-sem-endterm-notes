# Labour Law - Course Syllabus

**Course Title:** Labour Laws – I  
**Curriculum:** Industrial Relations, Dispute Settlement & Labour Codes  

---

## Course Objectives
To understand the evolution and constitutional foundation of labour law as an independent legal discipline, analyzing the statutory protections governing formal and informal workers, collective bargaining, conditions of service, employment security, and the transition toward the new Labour Codes.

---

## Statutory Framework & Legislative Codes
- **Key Statutes:**
  - The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
  - The Trade Unions Act, 1926
  - The Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946
  - The Factories Act, 1948
  - The Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948
  - The Minimum Wages Act, 1948 & The Payment of Wages Act, 1936
  - The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 & EPF Act, 1952
  - The POSH Act, 2013
- **The Four Labour Codes:**
  - The Industrial Relations Code, 2020
  - The Code on Wages, 2019
  - The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020
  - The Code on Social Security, 2020

## Detailed Course Modules

### Module 1: Origins and Evolution of Labour Law
- Historical emergence of labour law as a distinct legal discipline
- Changing idea of labour regulation; philosophical foundations of employment law
- India's labour force structure: Coexistence of formal and informal employment sectors

### Module 2: Constitutional & Common Law Roots
- Constitutional Mandates: Articles 14, 16, 19(1)(c), 21, 23, 24, 38, 39, 41, 42, 43, 43A
- Fundamental Human Rights & Labour: *PUDR v. Union of India* (Asiad Workers case); *Bandhua Mukti Morcha v. UOI*
- Role of ILO standards and international labour conventions

### Module 3: Trade Unions & Collective Bargaining
- Freedom of association and right to organize under the Trade Unions Act, 1926
- Registration, cancellation, rights, liabilities, and immunities of registered trade unions (Civil & Criminal immunity under Secs. 17 & 18)
- Political funds and general funds
- Trade union recognition under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020: Negotiating union and negotiating council

### Module 4: Industrial Conflict: Strikes, Lock-Outs & Boycotts
- Right to strike vs statutory right; legitimacy and legality of strikes and lock-outs
- General prohibitions and special procedures under public utility services (Sections 22, 23, 24 of IDA, 1947)
- Changes introduced under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (mandatory 14-day notice for all establishments)
- Consequences of illegal strikes and lock-outs: Wages during strike period (*Syndicate Bank v. K. Umesh Nayak*)

### Module 5: Regulation of Service Conditions & Standing Orders
- Framework, formulation, and certification of Standing Orders under the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946
- Model Standing Orders; terms of employment and modifications
- Standing orders framework under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (applicability threshold of 300 workers)
- Fixed-term employment (FTE) and statutory protections

### Module 6: Employment Security: Lay-off, Retrenchment, Transfer & Closure
- Definitions and distinctions: Lay-off [Sec. 2(kkk)], Retrenchment [Sec. 2(oo)], Closure [Sec. 2(cc)]
- Pre-conditions for valid retrenchment (Section 25F: Notice, retrenchment compensation, notice to appropriate government)
- Principle of "Last Come, First Go" (Section 25G) and re-employment rights (Section 25H)
- Special protections under Chapter V-B of IDA (applicability, prior government permission for lay-off, retrenchment, and closure)
- Judicial benchmarks: *Punjab Land Development v. Presiding Officer*; *Sundara Money*; *Uptron v. Shammi Bhan*

### Module 7: Industrial Dispute Resolution Machinery
- Concepts of "Industry" [Sec. 2(j)], "Workman" [Sec. 2(s)], and "Industrial Dispute" [Sec. 2(k)]
- *Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board v. A. Rajappa* principles and contemporary debates
- Dispute resolution authorities: Works Committees, Conciliation Officers, Boards of Conciliation, Courts of Inquiry, Labour Courts, Industrial Tribunals, and National Tribunals
- Reference power of appropriate government (Section 10) and Section 11A powers to set aside discharge/dismissal

### Module 8: Managerial Prerogatives & Protections
- Notice of change under Section 9A of IDA
- Protection during pendency of proceedings (Section 33, 33A)
- Protected workmen provisions and recovery of money due from employers (Section 33C)
